How the suggestions work (read once)

Data: RS3 only publishes one guide price and one daily volume per item, updated about once a day. There is no public live order book (unlike OSRS). The tool checks every 10 minutes for a new GE update and re-ranks when one arrives.

Spread flip: buy a little under the guide price, sell a little over it. The gap is a multiple of the item's day-to-day volatility over the last 30 days. Items whose gap can't beat the tax drop out on their own.

Dip recovery: the guide price sits well below its 30-day average. Buy near the guide and sell back towards the average. This takes days, and some dips are real drops, so watch the “falling” and “still dropping” flags.

Volume: quantity is capped by the 4-hour buy limit, your cash, and your share of the daily volume (default 5 %). It uses the lower of today's volume and the 30-day median, so a one-day spike doesn't inflate it. Fill time assumes the same share.

Tax: the seller pays the tax on each item, rounded down. Sales under 50 gp and Bonds don't pay it. Every profit figure is after tax.

Before you commit: do a margin check. Buy 1 at a very high price and note what it cost (the insta-buy). Sell it at a very low price and note what you got (the insta-sell). Enter both in “Check margin” to get real numbers.

Data: RuneScape Wiki GEMW & Weird Gloop API. Not affiliated with Jagex. Estimates, not financial advice — flipping always carries risk.
